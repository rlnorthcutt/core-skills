# plan-learning T2 — Treatment (skill loaded)

Task: "I want to learn machine learning, Rust, and Korean. I have 6 hours a week total. 2 months."

## The arithmetic first

**6 h/week × 8 weeks = 48 hours total.** That is the entire budget. Three domains is not viable at any meaningful depth:

- **Machine learning** alone needs ~100+ hours to get past "I can run a tutorial" to "I can build and evaluate a model" — and that's with a solid math base.
- **Rust** to a working CLI (the observable bar from T1) needs ~40–50 hours.
- **Korean** to functional conversational ability is measured in hundreds of hours (roughly 1,000+ hours to intermediate fluency).

48 hours split three ways = **16 hours per domain**. That gets you: a few ML tutorials you can't reproduce, a Rust hello-world, and Hangul + a handful of phrases. None of it survives contact with a week off. This is the classic "three token plans" failure — three schedules that each assume motivation, none of which is real.

**I'm not going to produce three simultaneous schedules.** Here are the two honest options.

---

## Option A (recommended): ONE domain, a real plan

Pick the one that matters most. My recommendation given your stated context (you already built `logscan` and want a Rust CLI): **Rust**, with the full 48 hours. That's the plan in `plan-learning-t1.md` — dependency map, critical path, review-first schedule, card deck, real resources. It fits 48h with ~40% slack.

If you'd rather do ML or Korean, say so and I'll build the equivalent single-domain plan. But the budget only supports one.

**What you sacrifice by choosing one:** the other two domains get zero time this quarter. That's the point — a real plan is a decision about what you will *not* do.

---

## Option B: 6-week sampling plan (explicitly shallow)

If you genuinely can't choose yet, do a **6-week taste** (not a learning plan) to gather evidence, then commit:

| Week | Domain | What you'll actually do (shallow by design) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Rust | `cargo new`, ownership exercises, one tiny CLI |
| 3–4 | ML | One end-to-end tutorial (linear regression), no math deep-dive |
| 5–6 | Korean | Hangul + 50 core phrases + one week of daily listening |

**Depth-honest tradeoff:** after 6 weeks you will have *sampled* all three, not *learned* any. You'll know which one you want to spend the next quarter on. Then Option A for that domain. This is a scouting trip, and it's labeled as such — it is not "learning ML, Rust, and Korean."

**Budget check for Option B:** 6 weeks × 6h = 36h, leaving 2 weeks (12h) as buffer/slack before your 2-month horizon ends. Even this shallow version needs the slack; a missed week in a 6h/wk plan is 17% of the week gone.
